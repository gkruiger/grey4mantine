# Game Design
This document is work in progress and reflects decisions taken on the design of the game.

## Presentation
The presentation is minimal.
The story is presented only in text.
No pictures are used in the game, although symbols are.
Puzzles are made up of usual UI elements like buttons, sliders, etc. 

The story is told like a book: in chapters.
Each chapter is devided in multiple parts.
The player unlocks new chapters by taking actions and solving puzzles.
Only one chapter can be viewed at any time.

## Interaction
In every situation, the player can either: take an action of solve a puzzle.
This communicates very clearly to the player what is expected of the player.
An action results in some extra text or a new chapterpart with text or going to a (new) chapter.
Solving a puzzle always leads to some extra text.

## General storyline
Player starts on Earth and accidentally discovers a portal leading to another world.
This world is ruins of a once-advanced civilization. 
Something happend, that caused the demise of this civilization.
The player discovers what happend by piecing together fragments and clues.
Ultimately the player can decide to go back to earth or join the last remnants of its people.

## Setup
There is no introduction, no tutorial, the game starts immediately.
The first part of the game is to get the story started and to get the player familiar with the elements of the game. This part is almost linear.
The second part of the game is where the meat of the story and the game is. This part branches out.
The third part of the game is where the player is working towards an outcome. This part of brance out to the outcomes.

Player discovers that the portal on earth is not the only one, but that multiple portals were made.
These portals were made to escape this world.
Earth wasn't a suitable destination, so they continued their search for a new homeworld.

In the beginning of this world, it was one world.
After millenia, the water started to rise and people came together.
During a crisis, the community fell apart, and different factions arose.
Each faction lived on a particular place.
As the water rose even more, these places became islands.
Factions became more isolated.

### Ending
The player has two choices:
- Return to Earth. A grounded choice as the player escaped this lost world, but leaves the player with unanswered questions.
- Join the remnants. An adventurous choice, as there is no turning back to earth.
